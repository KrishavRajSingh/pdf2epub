## What this changes

1. **Directory links are worth finishing after all, for this reason only.** They send almost no
   visitors (see `/funnel`), but KD 4 means ~5 real referring domains could put the homepage in the
   top 10 for a 10K/month search. Dofollow listings count most. AlternativeTo matters most.
2. **Portuguese and Spanish pages.** ~20K Brazilian and ~14K Spanish searches a month at KD 0.
   A real `/pt/` and `/es/` page (same converter, translated copy, `hreflang` tags, in the
   sitemap) is the cheapest traffic on this list.
3. **Track rank in Ahrefs Rank Tracker:** pdf to epub, convert pdf to epub, pdf to epub converter
   free, pdf to kindle, pdf para epub, converter pdf em epub, pdf a epub.
4. **Ahrefs Alerts** (new backlinks, new keywords, mentions of "epub.ftak.net") replaces
   Talkwalker/Google Alerts.
